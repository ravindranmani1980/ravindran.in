About

Infrastructure leadership built for regulated environments

I lead enterprise IT and cloud operations for GxP-regulated eClinical SaaS platforms, owning a multi-million-dollar technology portfolio and the 24x7 global operations behind it. Most of my career has centered on one problem: keeping complex, highly regulated systems reliable while they migrate, scale, and modernize underneath the people using them.

That includes leading the migration of 500+ critical servers to Azure and AWS, running FinOps practices that govern cloud spend and forecasting, and building disaster recovery and access-control programs that satisfy GxP and HIPAA requirements. As my work has moved deeper into security and compliance oversight, I'm extending that experience formally — currently working through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate to sharpen my grounding in threat detection, risk assessment, and incident response.
