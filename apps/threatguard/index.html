ThreatGuard

Cyber defense game for teens and young adults. Try it below: it runs in your browser with built-in sample data.

Problem
Young people meet phishing, scam DMs and fake Wi-Fi every day, but most security advice is passive.
Built
A game with hands-on drills for phishing and QR-code scams, rogue Wi-Fi, “compromised friend” DMs, password strength and a 45-second ransomware response, with a breakdown of every mistake.
Gives you
Practice spotting real attack patterns in a safe sandbox, with ranks, badges and progress tracking.
